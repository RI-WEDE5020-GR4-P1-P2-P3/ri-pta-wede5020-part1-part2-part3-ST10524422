Organisation Overview & History

EcoCare Community Trust is a registered South African non-profit organization established in 2018 in Gauteng. The organization was initially founded by a group of passionate environmental advocates aiming to clean up urban parks and assist underprivileged townships in establishing sustainable vegetable patches. Over the past eight years, EcoCare has grown into a structured trust managing multiple community-led initiatives, including urban tree planting, local household waste recycling drives, and educational school workshops on environmental conservation.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To empower local communities through accessible, sustainable environmental education and self-sustaining agriculture, directly combatting food insecurity in low-income urban areas.

Vision: To foster green, self-reliant South African urban communities where every neighbourhood possesses active recycling systems and sustainable food gardens.
